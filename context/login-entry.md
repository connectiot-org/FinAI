# Public log-in entry — 16 November 2026 (Asia/Dhaka)

The founder direction changed again after the demo-funnel release: the public site gets a visible **Log in** entry (branch `feat/login-entry`). Anyone can log in with an email and immediately use the connected workspace dashboard — Overview, accounting, receivables, payables, banking, close, reports and the FinAI Assistant. This entry-point request supersedes the "no public sign-in anywhere" rule from `request-demo-release.md` for the entry itself, while the demo-request funnel (`/request-demo`, Google Form embed) remains unchanged and keeps its role as the path for teams wanting their own provisioned workspace.

**Founder refinement, same day:** the `/login` card shows *only* the sign-in-to-your-workspace option. The "or" divider, the provisioned-customer `SessionGate` section (session builds) and the "request access through a demo" paragraph (default builds) were removed. Provisioned customers in session mode still reach their own gated workspace directly at `/workspace/overview` (its `Workspace.tsx` gate is untouched); the demo funnel stays available on every other public page, the sample tour and the workspace.

Scope:

- New public route `/login` (`frontend/src/features/marketing/LogIn.tsx` + `frontend/src/styles/log-in.css`) reusing the established welcome split-screen. The email form opens the honest browser-local Northstar demo workspace (`command {kind:"enter"}` → `/overview`) with a visible local-session note; the entered email only derives the workspace persona. Per the refinement above, the page no longer branches on `sessionEnabled` and shows only the email form plus the local-workspace note. No backend change: `/auth/*`, sessions and the paid workspace gate are untouched.
- Header, mobile navigation and footer expose the Log in entry (`Marketing.tsx`); `.marketing-login` header styles added in `marketing.css` (hidden below 990 px, where the mobile nav takes over, matching the previous header pattern). `/welcome` now redirects to `/login`; `/sample` keeps its account-free entry and gains a "Log in with your email" link. The homepage preview, product pages and every demo-request CTA are unchanged.
- Tests: `tests/marketing.spec.ts` updated — the login flow (header icon → `/login` → email → dashboard with full navigation), funnel separation, `/login` added to the public-page link audit (exempt from the demo-entry requirement as the focused entry card), and mobile navigation coverage. Other suites are untouched because workspace/checklist/assistant gates are unchanged.

Verification outcomes are recorded in `progress.md` under the same date.
