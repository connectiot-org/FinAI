# Public demo-request funnel — 16 November 2026 (Asia/Dhaka)

The founder changed the public go-to-market direction: the marketing site must not offer any sign-in option. Every former sign-in entry becomes a **Request a demo** call to action, backed by the team's Google Form (`https://forms.gle/qFhgN6g52xw4P17D7`). Workspaces stay provisioned by the FinAI team after a demo conversation; no public self-service registration exists. This supersedes the direction taken by commits `41b8bcb`..`c4f61af` (which had added public sign-in gates) while leaving the authenticated paid workspace product and its backend sessions untouched.

Scope of this change (branch `feat/request-demo-experience`):

- New public funnel route `/request-demo` (`frontend/src/features/marketing/RequestDemo.tsx`) with a premium hero, what-to-expect cards, numbered next steps, and the Google Form embedded in a site-styled browser-chrome card (`embedded=true&hl=en`), plus a new-tab fallback link. `/login` and `/welcome` redirect there.
- All public sign-in CTAs replaced with demo-request links across `Marketing.tsx`, `ReferenceHome.tsx`, `ProductPreview.tsx`, `ProductDetails.tsx`, `FinanceSnapshot.tsx` (hero dashboard, rail, stories, AI sections, integrations, badges, footers, menus). Constants live in `frontend/src/features/marketing/demo.ts`.
- Product catalogue extended to full reference-ERP parity: four new product pages — Continuous close, Audit & compliance, Implementation, Flexible GAAP reporting — and the Product menu restructured into the reference taxonomy (General ledger / Advanced accounting / Platform) via `productNavGroups` in `content.ts`. Solutions menu now covers By role (CFO, Controller, Accounting firms, Private equity), By company size (Startup, Mid-size, Enterprise) and By industry (six industries), each routing to the demo request.
- Premium motion layer without dependencies: `motion.tsx` (IntersectionObserver scroll reveal that clones its child, so no DOM wrappers break measured selectors) + `motion.css` (hero entrances, hover micro-interactions, bar growth, floating accents, grid drift; all transform/opacity, fully disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`). No layout geometry was changed.
- The `/sample` public workspace stays account-free; its workspace link now offers a demo instead of sign-in. The paid `/workspace` gate, `SessionGate`, `GoogleSignIn` and all backend auth remain for provisioned customers — no public page links to them anymore.
- Tests: `tests/marketing.spec.ts` rewritten for the new funnel (demo-request entries, embedded form, redirect, no public sign-in); other suites untouched because workspace/checklist/assistant gates are unchanged.
